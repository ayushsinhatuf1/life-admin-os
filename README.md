# Life Admin OS

Privacy-first, AI-assisted personal and family record intelligence.
Converts documents into structured, verified, connected records — and preserves
what families would otherwise lose between generations.

**This repository is a build kit, not a finished product.** Read `START-HERE.md`
first, then work through `PROMPT-PACK.md`.

## Scope boundary

Life Admin OS is a record-keeping, organisation, retrieval and intelligence
platform. It is not a government land-record system, a legal authority, an
inheritance adjudicator, a financial advisor, a claim adjudicator, or a valuation
authority. Ownership, inheritance, title and official valuation remain subject to
the relevant authorities and qualified professionals. Every ownership row in this
system is an association with a provenance note, never a statement of title.

## Reference frameworks

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework — AI trustworthiness and lifecycle risk
- OWASP ASVS — web application security verification
- India DPDP Act and DPDP Rules 2025 — privacy and compliance
